Metric definitions

Merchandise sales
Sum of quantity × unit price in GBP for positive purchases, excluding credit invoices and fee codes. This is not net revenue or profit.
Orders & average order value
Orders are distinct purchase invoice numbers. Average order value is merchandise sales divided by orders, including orders with missing customer IDs.
Active customers
Distinct nonmissing customer IDs purchasing during the selected period and market. Customers are counted directly for each slice, not summed across days.
Returning share
Identified active customers whose first observed purchase predates the reporting period, divided by all identified active customers in that slice. Earlier purchases may be in another market.
Period comparison
Monthly reporting compares with the previous month. Partial final-month reporting compares the same elapsed days. Full months use each month's complete calendar length; no trading-day adjustment. All-history reporting has no prior-period comparison.
Data boundaries
Observations run from 1 December 2009 through 9 December 2011. December 2011 is partial. Margin, stock availability, conversion and budget targets are not available in this dataset.
