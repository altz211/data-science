# Transaction data review

## Context & methods
Real UK gift-retailer transactions from 2009–2011. Source: Chen, D. (2012), Online Retail II, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D, CC BY 4.0.

### Key assumptions
Positive purchase sales exclude credits and nonpositive quantity/price. Missing customer IDs are retained for sales analysis. Identical line items are retained. This is not a margin or net-revenue analysis.

## Results
The dashboard uses the same exported monthly aggregates.
snapshot = json.loads((root/'web/assets/results.json').read_text())
monthly = pd.DataFrame(snapshot['monthly'])
monthly['month'] = pd.to_datetime(monthly['month'])
fig, ax = plt.subplots()
ax.plot(monthly.month, monthly.sales_gbp, color='#255f92', marker='o', markersize=3)
ax.set(title='Monthly positive purchase sales · UK retailer, 2009–2011', ylabel='Sales (GBP)', xlabel='Month')
ax.ticklabel_format(axis='y', style='plain')
fig.autofmt_xdate()
fig.tight_layout()
plt.show()
## Takeaways
The first and final months must be checked for incomplete coverage before comparing growth rates. Sales include customers without an ID; customer modelling uses only identified customers. The audit records excluded credits and the duplicate-retention policy.
